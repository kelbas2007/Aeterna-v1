## Scientific target

The first FRONTIER-1 sealed score was **FAIL 40/80**, because EvoPhase never chose active resampling. New TE1 phase-synapse evidence accumulator is physically causal, but the TE1 strict noisy held-out score still failed **63/80 vs preregistered ≥70**, with 11 4:4 abstentions. TE1 code/results remain preserved.

TE2 isolates the next **necessary**, but not sufficient, capability: learn which *opaque physical motor* actually generates an independent observable cue rather than doing nothing or terminating, and activate that motor's physical proposal when a native uncertainty state needs more evidence. It must not know that motor's semantic label.

## Mechanism boundary

- Opt-in on an existing TE1 physical evidence project. Cue source identities arise from raw observations; they are not user-supplied hidden categories.
- A factual `PRE, action, POST` trial is the only evidence for whether an opaque motor provides another distinguishable cue signal. The project grants sensory-affordance credit only for actual coherent transitions **between two previously acquired competing cue states**. An unchanged cue, third unrelated cue, unavailable POST, blocked protection action or fabricated observation grants no positive credit.
- Real motor-cell → physical project-cell `PhaseSynapse` carries learned action affordance. The readout must depend causally on phase conductance; untrained actions must not be mistaken for qualified sensors.
- When the physical evidence accumulator says `needs_more`, it may propose the best positively learned sensing affordance; when evidence is decisive or the episode's observation budget exhausted, abstain.
- The proposal is not an irreversible actuator command. If integrated later, Human Protection and shared U1 meta-control still have final say. TE2 itself is a native physical mechanism, not a host-selected module priority.
- Candidate motor identity is arbitrary opaque index; no world ID, answer motor, noise likelihood, hidden side or action reward label enters cognition.
- Checkpoint, lesion, π-shift and restore control must be real physical synapse interventions. Read-only probes may not change learned fingerprints.
- No claim that the organism will autonomously *discover* that motor without sufficient factual exposure or that a full noisy latent system passes. Sensor-search/budget arbitration remains for TE3.

## Generic prospective qualification

Using 6 opaque actions and two randomly assigned abstract raw sensory cues from an unrelated 24-state substrate:
- Every action receives matched factual PRE/action/POST exposure. Exactly one independently randomized action changes between the two cue identities; remaining actions leave the cue unchanged or transition to an unrelated third class.
- Shuffle action IDs and cue IDs across 6 arms; the correct sensor action must be selected in **6/6** arms solely from physically acquired link conductance.
- On ambiguous factual cue sequences, learned sensory action must be proposed; on sufficiently decisive multi-observation evidence or exhausted budget, no sensing proposal is allowed.
- Lesion and π-phase shift of the selected motor→project synapse suppress selection; exact restore returns selection, unrelated link lesion does not.
- All old TE1 physical behavior, G20–G23, U1–U3, and Human Protection regressions remain unchanged.

This is not a new FRONTIER-1 authority run, not a correction to consumed seeds and not proof of open-world AGI.
