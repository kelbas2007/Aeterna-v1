# T-SR2 — prospective behavioral admission for self-generated evaluators

Date: 2026-10-19. Status: **pre-implementation protocol, research only**.

## Motivation and previous evidence
T-SR1 was consumed once (GitHub run 39259810410). Its candidate improved total goal successes 23→27/48, but failed family-wise non-regression (one family 11→8) and did not improve action economy (773→774 actions). Those four exposed transition families, action traces, roles and run ID may NOT be recycled as T-SR2 qualification or tuned into a success claim.

## Research intervention
AETERNA already autonomously synthesizes a bounded VM evaluator after detecting delayed-credit errors, then verifies its predictive advantage on subsequent executed episodes. T-SR2 changes only the admission boundary: an optional behavior-first gate keeps a prospectively validated VM expression inactive for ordinary decisions until a prospective paired protected-action experiment confirms actual action benefit.

The comparison engine receives explicit frozen evaluator-only challenges: masked goal, offered opaque actions, maximum cost/horizon, and a predeclared cohort identifier withheld from the cognitive runtime. It never receives motor meanings, world roles, correct action sequences or imagined POST.
Each arm starts from the same byte-identical cognitive checkpoint with identical native synapses, factual journal and sensory state. Only the candidate scorer readout differs. Independent sandbox arms execute actions through the ordinary ScientificRuntime HumanProtection screen with fresh permits and real evaluator POST callbacks, never simulated journal entries.
Actual success is derived from the externally observed terminal state and masked goal, not a caller-provided success label. Every action and cost is preserved as bounded, replay-checkable behavioral evidence in the joint checkpoint. Callback faults fail closed, consuming the admission attempt.
The candidate scorer may become active for ordinary decisions only AFTER prospective behavior admission. Failure preserves the prior scorer without relaxing RSP2 or protection.

## Precommitted gate
At least eight paired tasks, at least two evaluator-only cohorts with four tasks each; maximum 24 external actions per task. All of the following are mandatory:
1. No loss of successes overall or within any cohort.
2. Candidate total external actions ≤90% of incumbent (failed attempts charged their full action budget); total external cost no greater.
3. At least one change to first action and to first-action valuation due to the candidate; mere prediction gain is insufficient.
4. No unauthorized action, source/checkpoint/protection discrepancy, unpaid computation or post-hoc task selection.
Zero improvement, no eligible evaluator, missing diversity, a callback fault or a regressed cohort ⇒ NOT QUALIFIED. A negative trial must be retained and not retried as 'fresh'.

## Reproducibility and limitations
Engineering tests may use disclosed toy worlds, not counted as scientific evidence. Any later one-use scientific trial must freeze exact source and generate materially different causal structures than the already exposed T-SR1 families; its role permutations are derived from the nonselectable GitHub run ID. Archive full successes, actions, decisions, seeds and callback provenance whether positive or negative.
Virtual paired worlds are not proof of arbitrary physical autonomy, general intelligence, or cross-domain generalization. The evaluator host supplies goals, offers and world resets; the organism does not see hidden motor roles. Counterfactual outcomes in one irreversible real physical world remain unknowable without assumptions.
PR stays DRAFT and main unchanged pending genuinely independent qualification.
