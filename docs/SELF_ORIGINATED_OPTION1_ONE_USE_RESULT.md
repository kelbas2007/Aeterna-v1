### Post-result independent trace inspection (exploratory, NOT pass rule)

The archived 32 paired world traces were inspected separately from
the published summary. **23 worlds improved, 9 tied, 0 regressed** in
successes. Physical actions improved in 23, tied in 9, regressed in 0.
No self-originated option was selected before its organism's first real
goal success in any world, and the direct-goal control produced no false
self-originated route. Across 32 paired world clusters, a 40,000-repeat
nonparametric world-level bootstrap gave *descriptive, post hoc* 95%
intervals: +214 to +370 successes across 32 worlds; roughly 45.1% to
63.7% action saving. This is not a preregistered significance test or
a certificate of general external-world validity.

## What did, and did not, emerge

A cold organism independently collects authorized sensorimotor evidence,
reinterprets its **own success** as a tentative executable short sequence
under contrasted real motor consequences, stores actual chronological facts,
selects options with its existing native U1, continues its own choice with
fresh real permits/POSTs, and updates option valuation only on genuine
finished outcomes. That causal loop is **end-to-end implemented and
successfully tested**, not merely a proposed feature.

The **algorithm and capacity for exactly THREE actions** were authored by
developers. There is still an external goal and world reset, one visible
numeric progress sensor, four opaque motors, and deterministic virtual
environments with small latent state. This trial does not demonstrate
discovery of arbitrary history depth, independent novel sensor variables,
language semantics, stochastic POMDP recovery, general-world tool use,
safe real-robot operation or autonomous editing of its own Rust/SNN architecture.
