## Replace action-history matching with relational belief formation

A developmental organism must be able to:
1. Segment candidate persistent entities from sensory continuity and action effects, without installing object names.
2. Bind a particular encountered entity to an episode-local identity token. Distinguish identity from appearance and location.
3. Maintain hypotheses under occlusion, with uncertainty and explicit possible identity loss.
4. Compare an earlier witnessed entity to a later encountered candidate, including controlled counterexamples.
5. Use the inferred relation to change its action, with a physical native SNN/phase carrier lesion showing necessity.

A proposed internal event is (subjective entity token, perceptual evidence, action, predicted outcome, actual outcome, confidence). Token identities must arise from experience and may be revised; no simulator hidden object IDs or task-specific class names may enter the agent.
