## Why this is bounded and generic

A candidate with evidence [0,0] may request an anchor on either observed side.
After obtaining one anchor fact on side A while side B remains 0, repeated side-A visits no longer justify the same experiment. When side B appears, that side is now under-sampled and the candidate may probe there.

Thus the rule allocates experiments toward missing hypothesis evidence rather than endlessly repeating an already sampled side.
