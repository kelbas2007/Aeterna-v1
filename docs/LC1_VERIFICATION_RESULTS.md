## Actual new capability

The **same ScientificRuntime and Learner checkpoint** holds acquired inverse
requirements and the existing RSP2 state. LC1 observes validated executed
triples `(operation, left, right, result)`. Without a hardcoded inverse
formula, the existing metered VM selects a right-hand inverse expression from
an authored generic arithmetic grammar. The selected rule can turn a desired
output into required intermediate values for a missing computation.

With no human sketch, LC1 enumerates bounded possible one-hole contexts,
infers the missing fragment, and **forward-executes the complete result** on
all support examples. An inverse is a proposed hint and cannot authorize
skipping the ordinary solver, executing a physical action, or adopting a
recursive policy. RSP3 candidate study and RSP2 producer/retention admission
are preserved as separate verification capabilities in the SAME organism.

## Limits that must remain visible

A supervised pure-VM mathematical construction demonstration is **not** an
open-world intelligence breakthrough. Grammar, observable operation names,
bounds, and examples are supplied; the SNN is not shown to invent the
operations. LC1 does not learn arbitrary nested or conditional inverses,
choose autonomous external research goals, or solve new public SyGuS cases.
Existing SyGuS 21-case and ARC-AGI-3 results are unchanged.

LC1 inverse rules are acquired computational knowledge, not automatic evidence
that the acquired RSP3 producer can create superior grandchildren.
Stable recursive self-improvement remains unproven.
