# SELF-PROBE — fully unforced acquisition of short hidden-causal context (development)

Date: 2026-10-19. Source base: faf084a153fe0d403a5dde8b741c14797ac82b09. This is **not** an independent AGI benchmark or science promotion.

## Evidence gap
AGENCY-CREDIT2 qualified on 32 source-disclosed virtual cases (22/32 vs 16/32; 426 vs 480 actions) but BOTH arms first received a **manually scheduled 133-step** physical action sequence. Although every POST was actually executed, the investigator selected the educational actions. The experiment does NOT prove that AETERNA discovers the causal prerequisite from a cold lifetime without tuition.

## Intervention
An optional U1-proposal-only `causal-pair-probe` uses the SAME executed Learner journal, no new authoritative executor, hidden-state oracle, motor semantics, forged POSTs or reset schedule. At an exact observed state with a preceding actually executed no-visible-change action, it distinguishes what it has vs has not tested among pairs (previous motor, candidate motor), and proposes the most under-evidenced safe candidate, subject to a bounded compute meter. This is an authored **experimental-design heuristic**, not an organism-created computation primitive; native U1 may decline it and protection always controls the motor.

The proposal cannot assert future success merely because a probe is unknown: goal score is zero; only observed uncertainty and novelty are credited. Prospective existing `calibrated_history_effect` keeps three chronological holdouts and matching-state contrasts before endorsing a causal rule. Cold causal probes are intended to *collect* those witnesses, not replace their gates.

## Exposed developmental test, fixed before observing new results
- Start ScientificRuntime cold, one persistent untrained Learner, 4 opaque available motors, one numeric progress sensor and an evaluator-only ephemeral hidden gate. Opt into agency-aware credit, factual control, short history and causal pair probes. Unlike prior AC2, **all four primitive motors remain available in every acquisition step**. The evaluator callback supplies only the actual POST and cost.
- Do not reset the external world between training steps and do not set a specific motor in the training schedule. A high, fixed goal keeps acquisition from halting after accidental first success.
- Use multiple role permutations and a corresponding cold same-initial-state control without the new probing option. Track all executed motor IDs, POSTs, count of native U1-selected probe proposals, number of controlled observed predecessor-conditioned pairs, work, safety calls, and budget.
- After the acquisition budget, expose a new goal at one unit above the organism's last ACTUAL progress and test whether a continuation action selected by U1 truly reaches the goal from the current hidden state. Both arms must be evaluated through protected POST actions; provide no action script.
- Diagnostic-only success: protected execution is genuine and uninterrupted, probe proposals selected sometimes, epistemic pair coverage differs, and acquired contextual prediction is tested against newly executed POSTs. Record negative outcomes without weakening the history-inference evidence gates.

## Research limits
The heuristic only considers one previous action and one visible state. It cannot learn arbitrary multi-stage hidden variables or discover freely what computation languages are available. Goal, options, external reset mechanics and sensors remain provided by host. No claim of autonomous self-improvement, arbitrary POMDP solution, or disjoint scientific transfer is allowed from this exposed test. Only after obtaining engineering evidence should an external-world protocol be preregistered *before* any further one-use trial.
