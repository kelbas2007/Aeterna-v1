## Generic execution rule
Bounded synchronous local propagation: a relay integrates its learned immediate outcome and discounted successor-cell value through learned phase-coherent conductances; local dendritic competition feeds the state-cell potential. After the fixed settling horizon, the observed receptor gates its acquired motor connections and motor competition produces the action. This is neural value propagation, not a claimed forward sensory rollout. Unknown/unformed/insufficiently coherent routes must abstain.

No evaluator state/action names enter production. Prototype recognition remains an inherited HDC prior; P1 does not qualify new representation learning or nuisance robustness. Capacity and horizon are bounded.
