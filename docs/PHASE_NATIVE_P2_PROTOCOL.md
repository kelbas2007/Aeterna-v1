## Objective

Extend P1 beyond backward outcome-value propagation. An opaque action and present acquired state must cause forward activation of successor cells and generation of a predicted relational phase vector through learned synapses in the existing EvoPhase cell/synapse arrays. The resulting activity, not an evaluator-provided intermediate observation, must continue a multi-action imagined trajectory. An ordinary agent must choose an action, predict before seeing POST, execute, compare the factual observation and revise from facts.

This is a bounded learned forward world-model mechanism, not a claim of general intelligence, raw-image generation, self-invented physical laws or autonomous open-world curriculum.

## Ownership boundary

No LLM, external service, imported transition table or legacy graph planner in the P2 path. Input prototype recognition remains inherited. Forward continuation must not copy a successor receptor's stored trace, ask an evaluator for a future state, or enumerate routes. The learned phase-vector decoder must use actual shared synapse weights/phase offsets; structural metadata may contain addresses and evidence counts, not a second vector-valued prediction table.

Phase/charge activity advances through action-gated afferent, successor and decoder connections. The finite update rule is inherited. Do not describe a synchronous phasor simulation as biological oscillation/spiking or self-invented reasoning. P1 action value propagation may choose the actual motor; its origin must remain explicit.

REAL observations, factual tick and learned parameters must not change during imagined activity. Unknown inputs, unsupported actions and disconnected paths must abstain rather than use a hidden fallback. P2 is opt-in and does not relabel G8/G9 or P1 results.

## Acquisition and evaluation

Use raw numeric rasters and opaque motor IDs. Train only individual factual transitions, not whole successful route scripts. Training covers the small deterministic transition set; unseen full trajectories compose 2–5 learned steps. New absolute raster bindings are absent from tuition. This is composition and spatial-binding evaluation, not discovery of wholly untrained transition laws.

Use a fixed maximum of 160 presentations per transition, reporting all presentations. Same facts/order go to matched controls. Freeze learning for evaluation. Report prediction cosine similarity to independently encoded actual POST and to the alternative-action POST. Require >=0.97 for every intended step and end-to-end evaluator-confirmed Need in the intact deterministic mechanism cases.

## Causal interventions

1. Necessary internal successor conductance zero: continuation fails at that link, not before the valid prefix.
2. Necessary phase offset shifted by pi with weights/topology fixed: continuation fails.
3. Exact restoration without learning: entire predicted trajectory recovers.
4. Decoder-only phase perturbation: predicted vector is corrupted while input prototypes remain untouched; restoration recovers. This rules out copying stored target vectors.
5. Unrelated connection lesion: intended trajectory remains correct.
6. Acquisition with zero phase learning, zero weight learning, or no available cells: no full predictive capability.
7. Learning frozen during factual revision: learned fingerprint remains unchanged and obsolete prediction persists.

## Factual revision

In a fully observed deterministic world, change the successor of one known state/action. New factual observations may locally suppress the contradicted connection and form a newly supported successor circuit, preserving old circuit IDs and contradiction evidence. Report the changed and unchanged routes separately. Do not call this arbitrary stochastic hypothesis revision or preservation of one entire rewired program. No imagined forecast is accepted as factual tuition.

## Required integrated witness

One acquired EvoPhase instance, with no graph backend, must select actions through the P1 native readout, form the next-state forecast before each physical action, receive raw factual POST, report prediction error, and reach delayed factual Need. After a changed-law observation sequence it must forecast the revised consequence, retain the unchanged route, and outperform a frozen-learning copy on that changed transition. The evaluator may implement the world and score outcomes, but not choose the organism's action.

## Validation and evidence policy

Deterministic tests: all six permutations of three opaque motor labels and route lengths 2,3,4,5, plus decoder-causality, abstention, revision, REAL isolation, and source-boundary tests. These are mechanism/regression evidence only.

Optional fresh qualification: only after a frozen source/spec and first-attempt GitHub run ID authority. Ten run-derived sub-seeds, eight instances each, all generated parameters and digest printed before acquisition/scoring. Minimum 76/80 complete forecasts and integrated successes, Wilson lower bound >0.88, >=6/8 each sub-seed; causal-control counts and every failure reported. The generated instances share one narrow world family and may repeat configurations. A development seed or reused pack must never be presented as fresh.

Compile/regression/release results are reported independently from scientific acceptance. Preserve unsuccessful attempts; no automatic old-fresh-suite consumption or continuous CI. End the work with CI manual-only. If fresh qualification is not executed, report MECHANISM_ONLY or BUILD_ONLY precisely.

## Remaining larger objective

A useful intelligence still requires autonomous experience collection, acquired state distinctions under ambiguity, transfer beyond a supplied transition set, goal conditioning, efficient continual learning, persistence, and unified cognitive ownership. P2 addresses a missing forward-model component and its ordinary action/fact integration; it does not redefine the full objective as passing a small test.
