## Diagnosis

Both `phase_compositional_sidecar` and the direct compositional observation path prohibit all later hypotheses on a base after the *first* one is born, even if it has retired from negative evidence. The earlier development remainder reported a rejected Atom and no alternative XOR candidate.

## Permitted general change

1. A base with active non-retired compositional candidates continues evidence collection without competing new births.
2. When that base has no active candidates, search the bounded *factual discovery* history for a same-base, same-opaque-action, contradictory-successor pair with differing raw features.
3. Generate candidate programs only using the existing depth-2 Atom/And/Xor grammar and `candidate_programs`, preserving generic ordering and limits.
4. An already attempted structural candidate (base, anchor action, candidate program and unordered factual successor pair) may never be born again, **including when retired**. Retired provenance is retained.
5. After prior hypotheses on the base, recruit at most one new distinct candidate per collision; first-time discovery keeps the existing behavior and G23 regression.
6. Preserve all future-only evidence gates, promotion thresholds, physical cell/synapse ownership, max candidate count, Human Protection and one-POST-one-parent-commit.
7. Apply the identical birth policy to direct and fan-out observation paths. Do not use world IDs, true program labels, correct actions or evaluator outcomes as hints.

## Falsifiable gates

- Structural regression: initial rejected candidate remains stored and retired, later genuinely distinct candidate is born; no exact retired duplicate and no unlimited spawning.
- Independent future evidence on the successor must promote a useful alternative; lesion/phase perturbation should eliminate its readout and restore recover.
- Existing G23 and integrated G20–G23 controls, U1–U3 and Human Protection must not regress.
- Burned World-D and remainder-world replay are development *diagnostics*, not a new INTEL verdict; record even if they remain FAIL.

A passing local gate qualifies only hypothesis succession. It does **not** qualify general autonomous developing intelligence or prove the full D/E system chain.
