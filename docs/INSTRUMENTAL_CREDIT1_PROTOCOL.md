# INSTRUMENTAL-CREDIT1 — distinct epistemic acquisition versus goal-control learning

Date 2026-10-19. Pre-implementation, single-use research protocol. Current baseline is SELF-PROBE development result in run 39313546099. Research only.

## Contradiction motivating this intervention
The new cold organism CHOSE 57 causal pair experiments from native U1 without a tuition schedule and observed 23 actual progress steps across two cold worlds (versus 14 with the previous control). Nevertheless, on one world it failed the immediate successor goal despite 24 offered, chronologically verified history plans that native U1 never selected. The same goal was achieved in 2 actions by the unmodified arm. This is **negative behavioral transfer** and may not be reclassified as a qualified improvement.

The proposed mechanism is a source-independent, PRE/POST-grounded separation between *epistemic evidence acquisition* and *instrumental outcome credit*. No extra motor/action executive, semantic role oracle, pretrained model, or hand-injected U1 synaptic weights.

## Precommitted intervention
Opt in at the start of a fresh construction runtime, alongside agency-aware U1 credit, before the first action. World factual learning proceeds on EVERY valid protected real PRE/action/POST transition. For U1's **instrumental** meta-value:
- Only an actually executed self-selected action that makes **measured positive progress** toward a masked goal may receive immediate goal-policy credit. Merely discovering an unfamiliar POST state does NOT count as accomplishing the goal.
- A learned goal-reaching history proposal that changes no visible progress is an eligibility trace, NOT a zero-reward failure. If the next protected action actually produces goal progress, it may earn retrospective credit. Its credit must be bound to the prior real action, unchanged goal and chronological adjacency; revoked on interruption, goal change, veto or failed next action.
- Unknown-action experiments that yielded no goal progress continue supplying causal facts, but cannot train the *same U1 goal utility* as an instrumental success. Self-initiated epistemic actions retain their separate observable count (not a reward invented from unexecuted predictions).
- Until adequate physical goal evidence exists, zero native instrumental evidence is an explicit abstention, not a fabricated learned value. No automatic goal-rank inflation of a proposed plan; native U1 still arbitrates the motor and HumanProtection still screens every physical act.

Backward compatibility: default remains OFF. The opt-in is one-way, persistent in the joint checkpoint, and cannot be enabled in an already-trained or frozen cognition. No change to RSP2 admission and no relaxation of protection.
 
## Exposed engineering falsifiers
- Unforced actions with zero real goal progress must NOT alter U1 instrumental weights in new mode, even if reward novelty or a hypothetical plan score is high; the exact factual journal still records them.
- A later verified goal advance MUST update the U1 evidence learning, and a preceding independently selected validated history prerequisite receives its eventual goal credit only when the next protected POST confirms progress.
- HumanProtection veto cannot mint a policy label or a synthetic POST.
- In the same source-frozen binary, baseline with option OFF stays semantically identical to previously passing tests.

## Independent future scientific challenge
On a genuinely distinct causal-world family (NOT the exposed pair fixture or the AC2 4 families), start both arms cold with all motors offered, and let U1 choose every motor during training and solving. Compare old evidence-agnostic immediate value versus this prospective instrumental-credit opt-in. Freeze code and formal criteria before the first run. Minimum 4 cohorts x 8 hidden-role cases; require no cohort-success regression, >=10% fewer total executed actions at equal budgets, plus actual U1-selected verified history continuations. Any failure is a preserved NEGATIVE and cannot justify republishing an exposed cohort as fresh.

**Limitations:** The intervention and history window are developer-authored, not self-invented. A positive engineering test does not imply general recursive self-improvement or cross-domain transfer.
