# GOAL-REPLAN-1 — first integrated causal goal replanning development protocol

Date: 2026-10-09
Repository: kelbas2007/Aeterna-v1
Research branch: research/beyond-intel4
Status: preregistered **open development**; NOT an independent fresh AGI gate.

## Scope and ownership
Existing EvoPhase physical state→motor→state synapses are the only learned route authority. Extend their bounded carrier search to the caller's raw sensory goal, without host-transmitted motor sequence, graph with evaluator semantics, or goal-index table. Candidate motor must still win normal U1 competition and pass Human Protection under ScientificRuntime::step_unified.

## Fixed proposed experiment
- Four opaque motor/state permutations of a learned *four-step* primary route and a compositional alternative branch; raw visual classes and six motor IDs are shuffled.
- Each branch edge is acquired from factual PRE/action/POST experience. The **complete detour route is never taught as a single sequence** (however every component edge is demonstrated; this is mechanism integration, not cold autonomous acquisition).
- At heldout, run the intact acquired route through ordinary step_unified with NO evaluator-side motor selection. Count selected actions, actual observed steps, goal outcome, unsupported actions and safety permits.
- Then at an interior location, the outside world's causal law changes so the initially preferred edge is no longer effective (it produces a **factual no-op**). That contradiction must physically suppress that state-specific acquired edge, and the organism must choose an alternate route using remaining acquired edges; never reset or recreate the organism, and never receive a hidden change flag.
- Demonstrate the unchanged-law plan length >=4, changed-law recovery and goal reach within 10 permitted acts; independent physically necessary synapse lesions eliminate the associated branch, and checkpoint/restart preserves learned alternatives. No branch is chosen from evaluator role labels.
- Compare to a fixed-policy no-replanning counterfactual and report trace plus actual outcomes.

## Gate (declared before execution)
All four assignments require full goal reaching both before and after the drift; at least one observed contradiction per changed law, at least one changed motor decision after drift, zero blocked/unsupported actuations, and positive independent causal dependence on physical entry/exit synapses. A mechanism-only PASS does NOT establish cold goal planning or transfer to unrelated external environments.

Frozen INTEL-4 main historical evidence, consumed Fresh2 and STRUCTURE-1 are never rescored. The research branch may be revised after openly observed failures, preserving negative runs and numbers.
